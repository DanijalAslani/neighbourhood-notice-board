Thursday · 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.

Planned water cut-off on Maple Street

The water company will repair a pipe on Thursday, June 19. Homes between Oak Road and Pine Lane may have no water from 9 a.m. until about 1 p.m.

Before the water is off

  1. Fill a few clean bottles for drinking and cooking.
  2. Set aside water for handwashing and pets.
  3. Turn off taps and check that washing machines are not running.

For official updates, visit the EPA WaterSense website (opens in a new tab).

Free · Tuesdays after school

Free homework club at the library

Need a hand with homework? Local volunteer Maya is hosting a relaxed study hour every Tuesday from 3:30 to 4:30 p.m. in the Maple Street Library meeting room.

Bring your books, questions and a snack. The club is free for students in grades 4–8; a parent or guardian should sign children in on their first visit.
